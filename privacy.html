Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What data is collected

  • Analytics — This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to measure aggregate traffic (pages viewed, session duration, general location). No personally identifiable information is stored.
  • Contact form — When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to Formspree and forwarded to G-Su Paek's email. This data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Visitor counter — An anonymous page-view count is stored in Supabase. No IP addresses or personal data are recorded.

Cookies & local storage

The site stores a theme preference and a session flag (to avoid double-counting your visit) in your browser's localStorage/sessionStorage. No advertising or tracking cookies are set by this site.
