WHAT
COACHING
LOOKS LIKE

Whether you're a brand-new troupe still figuring out your voice or an established group looking to level up, I meet you where you are. Sessions are hands-on, direct, and built around your ensemble — not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Expect honest notes, real reps, and a coach who's been in the ensemble trenches right alongside you.

Ensemble Work

Group scenes, listening drills, and pattern-breaking exercises designed to make your troupe feel like one organism on stage.

Scene Deconstruction

Watch it back together. I break down what worked, what didn't, and exactly how to fix it — without the jargon.

Consistent Sessions

Regular coaching builds habits. I'm open to ongoing relationships with troupes who want dedicated, long-term development.
